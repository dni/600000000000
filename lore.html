THE NUMBER

In Riga in 2022, DNI met a nameless Bitcoiner in a red cap. The stranger said he had sold everything in 2010, bought bitcoin and timelocked it for 21 years. He believed one bitcoin would reach 600 000 000 000. He did not expect to live to see it. He imagined his grandchildren buying spaceships with the stack.

It was never a price prediction. It was patience taken to an absurd, beautiful extreme. The stranger walked away. DNI carried the story to Austria.

THE MEME

At the first gathering in Austria were dni, sat, shillie, michael1011 and flx. And there was Oink, a wild boar.

Oink memed 600 Billion into existence. The number stopped being somebody else's story and became a living culture: people, jokes, artifacts, code, music and games made in the open.

Oink is our Satoshi.

Not a boss. Not a prophet. The origin nobody owns. Oink later disappeared. We lost the boar, not the signal, and the search continues through everything we build.

THE CULTURE BEFORE THE PALACE

The Palace of Culture needs time. It will not be ready before Christmas 2026, so THE BUILDER releases two pieces of its culture first.

The 600B Timelock TCG turns the world into cards, characters, resources and choices. People can play the rules of the Palace before its doors exist.

DJ David Clanker is the second culture asset: a two-deck DJ Napplet for Wavlake, Nostr playlists and local audio files. It lets people hear, mix and curate the world before it has a physical dancefloor.

One carries the rules. The other carries the rhythm. By Christmas the Palace should not be an empty monument searching for an audience. The culture will already be alive.

We have no headquarters. Nostr carries identity and conversation. GitHub carries the build. Lightning carries value directly between people. The characters of join.600.wtf enter the tale one by one, through what they make and do.
